Digital Trust Infrastructure for Vehicle Lifecycle Records

Trust around every vehicle, built together.

Auto Trust Layer is a collaborative digital trust infrastructure where authenticated vehicle owners, authorised service stations and ecosystem participants contribute verifiable events to build a continuously evolving layer of trust around every vehicle.

Trust is not created by a single organisation. It emerges when every participant contributes authenticated information to a common layer of truth.

Every authenticated interaction strengthens the trust layer surrounding a vehicle throughout its lifecycle.

Currently under controlled pilot deployment.

The Auto Trust Layer

A common foundation for trusted vehicle lifecycle information.

The vehicle itself does not create trust. Trust is accumulated through authenticated actions performed by independent participants over time.

01

Verified identity

Every important action is attributable to an authenticated human or institution.

02

Verified provenance

Every event records who created it, where it originated and when it occurred.

03

Owner-controlled trust

The verified owner reviews and approves completed records before final vault acceptance.

04

Lifecycle continuity

Trusted events accumulate around the vehicle from ownership through service and eventual end of life.
